## 📌 About the Project

This project presents an interactive business intelligence dashboard built with Microsoft Power BI to monitor business performance from 2024 to 2026. It transforms sales data into clear visual insights to support performance evaluation and data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Monitor key revenue and profitability indicators.
- Analyze monthly revenue and profit trends.
- Identify top-performing products and categories.
- Compare revenue across sales channels.
- Evaluate regional sales performance against targets.
- Provide an interactive overview of business performance.

## 📈 Key Dashboard Features

- **KPI Overview:** Total Revenue, Total Profit, Total Orders, Average Order Value, and Target Achievement.
- **Revenue & Profit Trends:** Monthly performance analysis.
- **Product Analysis:** Revenue comparison across individual products.
- **Sales Channel Analysis:** Performance comparison across different sales channels.
- **Category Analysis:** Revenue, quantity, profit, and profit margin comparisons.
- **Regional Performance:** Actual revenue versus sales targets by region.
- **Interactive Filters:** Explore performance by year, region, category, sales channel, and customer segment.
